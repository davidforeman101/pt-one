What makes it different

Honest is a feature.

PT‑One doesn't guilt-trip you, inflate your progress, or invent a wellness score to keep you tapping. The app shows you your data and lets you draw the conclusions — it never pretends to know your body better than you do.

No streak guilt

Take a week off and your streak bridges it. Time away is life, not failure — challenges pause and extend instead of collapsing.

No made-up scores

If a number would be a guess, we don't show the number. Plain figures, real evidence, and a recovery clock you control.

No dark patterns

No nagging, no fake urgency, no "one weird trick". We'd rather under-promise than over-coach — the honesty is the brand.

Plans that think

Show up. The numbers are already set.

Programs handle the arithmetic — what to lift today, when to add weight, when to hold steady. Targets turn a goal into a numbered route. Challenges give a month a shape. Your job is the reps; if life gets in the way, the plan bends. It never guilt-trips.

  • Built to your goal — get-stronger, build-muscle and keep-moving programs are genuinely different: different lifts, different shapes, different numbers.
  • Progressive overload, explained — a nudge when a lift has sat still, and a plain "why" behind every suggestion.
  • Real medals — records and milestones minted from your actual history, never participation confetti.

Programs

Multi-week plans with the numbers pre-set and a lighter week built in — starting strength to volume blocks, plus programs Petra builds around you.

Targets

"Get stronger" isn't a plan. "Session 9 of 24 on the road to your first chin-up" is. Pick the goal, see the whole route before step one.

Challenges

One clear thing a day for a month. Life intervenes? It pauses and extends. The only way to fail is to stop forever.

Know your body

See what's ready before you train.

Every session paints a body map — which muscles you worked, and how recovered they are now, on a clock you control rather than a formula we hide. Glance before you train and pick the day that fits: legs are fresh, chest needs another day.

  • Pain, tracked — never diagnosed — where it hurts, how much, alongside what you trained. Patterns land in your data, not your memory.
  • Sessions that respect it — flagged areas ease the plan and rank kinder swaps first, with a plain reason every time.
  • A weekly review worth reading — sessions, records, muscles worked and honest observations. Silence on a quiet week; that's the trust.
