Hands-free guided sessions

It speaks. You move. That's the whole deal.

Mid-stretch, on the floor, hands full — that's exactly when tapping a phone fails you. PT-One runs the session for you, out loud, from the first "get ready" to the last "done".

  • Every move announced — the exercise, the cue, the count-in.
  • Every rep and hold timed — with rests handled and both sides guided one at a time.
  • Talk back if you like — "log eight", "skip the rest", "where are we?" — and it answers.
  • The whole session on your wrist — cues, timers and heart rate on Apple Watch while the phone charges in another room.
From first stretch to strength

Starts gentle. Grows serious. Stays yours.

PT-One began as one person's way to actually do their back exercises — because following the clinic's sheet was the hard part, not the exercises. The app that walks you through gentle guided movement will happily run your five-rep squat day: sets, weights, rests, supersets, progress over time, with the same voice carrying you the whole way.

  • Load your plan once — every session after that is just pressing play.
  • Rehab isn't a separate world that ends — it's where training begins.
  • You don't graduate to a different app — PT-One is built to be outgrown into, not out of.
What makes it different

Honest is a feature.

PT-One doesn't guilt-trip you, inflate your progress, or invent a wellness score to keep you tapping. The app shows you your data and lets you draw the conclusions — it never pretends to know your body better than you do.

No streak guilt

Take a week off and your streak bridges it. Time away is life, not failure — challenges pause and extend instead of collapsing.

No made-up scores

If a number would be a guess, we don't show the number. Plain figures, real evidence, and a recovery clock you control.

No dark patterns

No nagging, no fake urgency, no "one weird trick". We'd rather under-promise than over-coach — the honesty is the brand.

Everything inside

Deep where it counts, simple where it matters.

One app, one voice, and a lot underneath it — every piece built to the same rule: it works for you, plainly, or it isn't in.

Petra — the coach in your corner

Ask for a change. Get a session.

Petra is the coach inside PT-One: ask her to build a session for the kit you have, swap an exercise that isn't working, or explain why today looks the way it does — and the answer lands as a real, playable session, not a paragraph of advice.

You: "Swap the rows — the bar's taken."
Petra: "Done — seated cable row, same sets, same weight curve."
You: "Only got dumbbells today. Make it shorter."
Petra: "Rebuilt: five exercises, thirty-five minutes. Press play."
  • Works the moment you install — no key, no account, no set-up.
  • Honest about what she is — and she never tells you to push through pain.
  • Hands the wheel straight back — every change is yours to keep, tweak or undo.
Private by design

Your training data isn't our business model.

Your sessions, your history, your pain notes — all of it lives on your iPhone, backed up sealed to your Apple ID. No account to create, nothing to harvest. Ask Petra for help and even that stays nameless — your question travels with no identity attached and isn't kept. She answers; it's gone.
